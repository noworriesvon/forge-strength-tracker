▥ Barbell Strength & Wilks Scores

Date-stamped actual 1RM tests, weekly bodyweight, individual-lift Wilks2 or Original Wilks, and 1–10 rep estimates. Scores are unofficial single-exercise adaptations, not competition totals.

⚖ Weekly Bodyweight

New weeks automatically inherit the previous week's weight. Historical weeks initially use the profile weight, labeled Assumed. Edit any week to replace it with a measured weight.

◈ Bodyweight Exercise Records

Pull-ups, push-ups and dips: enter 0 as added weight for an unweighted set, or a positive additional load for a weighted set. Related unassisted grips share family records; assisted and modified variants stay separate.
